# TennisOS

### From Hypothesis to Evidence.

**Independent Research in Applied Machine Learning, Predictive Analytics and In-Play Tennis Modeling**

---

## About TennisOS

TennisOS is an independent research and development initiative exploring the intersection of professional tennis, machine learning, probabilistic forecasting and market dynamics.

Unlike conventional pre-match prediction systems, TennisOS focuses on a specific decision point within a match: the transition into the decisive third set of a best-of-three encounter.

The central research question is:

**Can information generated during the first two sets provide a measurable predictive advantage beyond the probabilities implied by the market?**

## Research Scope

- ATP Tour
- WTA Tour
- ATP Challenger Tour

Our research combines:

- Proprietary feature engineering
- Random Forest and Neural Network architectures
- Probabilistic modeling and market benchmarking
- Walk-forward evaluation
- Prospective out-of-sample validation
- Automated in-play data acquisition

## Scientific Philosophy

TennisOS is built around a fundamental principle:

**A predictive advantage is not established by historical performance alone. It must survive prospective validation under real-world conditions.**

Our research emphasizes:

- Temporal integrity and prevention of data leakage
- Transparent evaluation methodology
- Model versioning and reproducibility
- Separation of research and production environments
- Evidence over assumptions
- Documented limitations and negative results

## 2027 — From Hypothesis to Evidence

The 2027 tennis season is planned as a dedicated prospective validation period.

The research framework includes frozen reference models, timestamped signal documentation, market benchmarks and controlled Champion–Challenger experiments.

The objective is not to promise profitability, but to determine whether a sustainable and executable predictive advantage exists.

## Development & Architecture

TennisOS is evolving towards a modular, provider-independent architecture combining predictive intelligence, automated data acquisition, decision support and systematic performance monitoring.

**TennisOS owns the intelligence. The provider supplies the data.**

## Public Research & Publications

This GitHub organization will serve as a public platform for selected research documentation, methodological discussions, validation frameworks and technical insights.

Core models, proprietary datasets, feature definitions and production decision rules remain private.
